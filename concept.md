# Concept

A 4X game but at a lower scale and with magic, focused around units that have equipements, levels and skills.

Inspiration: `Battle for Wesnoth`, `Millennia`

The terrain is hex tiles of the size of a building, so the entire map is a region, not a continent.

The units arn't on fixed types, they are basic first but we can specialise them with tools or weapons and give them skills or stats as they level up.
For convenience, some preset can be trained one the necessairy conditions are met.

## Equipements

- building tools: allow to build, upgrade the buildings, and boust the production of the building of the cell
    - farm: produce food each turn
    - wall: boost defence
    - castle: boost defence + allow to recruite units
    - forge: allow to create equipements
    - wood cutting camp: produce wood
    - quary: produce stone
    - mine: produce iron
    - crystal mine: produce cristals used for magic
    - library: produce research points
    - house: produce golds, can recruit basic unit and commoners
    - training ground: give xp to the unit, can recruit fighters and archers
    - magic building: produce mana, allow to cast global spells, can recruit mages
- spear/sword: allow to fight efficently
- bow: allow ranged attacks
- magic staff/wand: boost magic
- cloth / armor: boost defence
- accessories (rings, amulets, etc.): provide various bonuses (e.g., increased mana, improved stats)
- mount: provide increased movement speed and combat advantages on flat terrain.

## Unit progression

When an unit level up, it get more hp and the player get to chose between a fiew options.

- a stats improvement
- a efficiency multiplier (ex: better farming, attack, mana gathering, ...)
- a passive skill (ex: produce bonus ressource, poison, ...)
- an active skill (not only magic, it can be unloking a building construction)

The options have random factors but are mostly based on the sources off the experience points of the level.
(civil/fighting/magic/exploration/leader)

## Leader

The player lose when his leader die.
Get 30% more exp.

Can build and move the castle.
When on the castle:
- can recruit units
- small boost to the production of other buildings

## Research

Research allows the player to unlock buildings, upgrade existing structures, get passive bonuses, or unlock global spells (witch mostly cost mana and can provide buffs, heals, summons, or deal damage; range depend on the spell, passives bonuses, and of the unit that cast if from the magic building).
